Our story

DANIEL started with a simple idea: build a small wardrobe of pieces you reach for every week — soft, durable, and honestly priced.

We work with makers who care about fit and fabric hand-feel. Each season we release slowly, restock what people love, and leave the rest behind.

From Yangon to the rest of Myanmar, we ship with care and stand behind every order.

Quality first

Fabrics chosen for longevity, not just look — washed, worn, and worn again.

Fair pricing

Transparent costs without seasonal markup games or fake “was” prices.

Local delivery

Reliable shipping across Myanmar with clear timelines and easy returns.

How we make things

  • 1. Choose fabric — we pick materials that feel good on day one and year three.
  • 2. Fit for real life — cuts with room to move, not runway exaggeration.
  • 3. Small drops — we release slowly, listen, and restock winners.
