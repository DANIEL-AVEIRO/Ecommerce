FAQ

Quick answers about orders, shipping, returns, and your account.

Orders & payment

What payment methods do you accept? +

Cash on delivery, KBZ Pay, Wave Money, and bank transfer. Choose your method at checkout.

Can I cancel an order? +

Yes — message us as soon as possible with your order number. If it hasn’t shipped yet, we’ll cancel and refund.

How do I track my order? +

Sign in, open Orders, and view the order detail page for status updates.

Shipping

How long does shipping take? +

Yangon: 2–4 days. Major cities: 3–5 days. Other regions: 4–7 days. Express options are available at checkout.

When is shipping free? +

Standard shipping is free on orders over 150,000 Ks. Details on our shipping page.

Returns & product

Can I return or exchange an item? +

Unused items with tags can be returned within 7 days. See our returns policy.

How do sizes fit? +

Most pieces are true to size with a relaxed everyday cut. If you’re between sizes, size up. Ask us anytime for a fit note.

Do you restock sold-out sizes? +

Often yes. Join the newsletter or save the item to your wishlist.

We’re happy to help with order or product questions.
